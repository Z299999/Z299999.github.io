Van Build — Chevy Express

2024–present · ongoing self-build · learning by hand

I am converting a Chevy Express into a camper, doing the work myself — framing, insulation, electrical, cabinetry, and finish. This page documents the build as it grows. I take it on to learn more hands-on craft, to meet others who make things and care about creative work, and to build toward making more meaningful things over time.
